# Procedures

## Requesting a Deferred Exam
1. The student messages their course professor through the app to initiate a deferral request.
2. The professor sends a deferral request form to the student via in-app messaging.
3. The student completes and submits the form, attaching any required supporting documentation.
4. Requests should be submitted as soon as possible; delays may affect the outcome.

## Review and Decision
5. The faculty member reviews the submitted deferral request.
6. Faculty may approve or deny the request; the decision is communicated to the student
   through the app.
7. Faculty retain the ability to approve or deny a request even after it has been submitted.

## Room Booking (Admin)
8. Admin books available exam rooms throughout the semester on an ongoing basis.
   Room bookings are independent of individual deferral decisions and are made proactively
   to accommodate expected demand across midterms, tests, quizzes, and finals.

## Scheduling the Deferred Exam
9. When completing the deferral request form, the student selects their preferred
   availability from the dates and times of rooms already booked by admin. Students
   may only choose from these pre-booked options.
10. The AI assistant can help a student choose suitable dates if the student shares their
    course schedule or deadline information. The assistant will reason over the student's
    commitments against the available options visible to them in the form.
11. Faculty review the student's chosen availabilities — which already align with
    admin-booked rooms — and select one to confirm as the final exam time. No additional
    room availability check is required at this stage.
12. Faculty hold final say over the scheduled time; the student does not unilaterally
    determine the date or time.

## Exam Day
13. The student checks in on exam day using their personal QR code, scanned by the proctor.
14. A manual check-in override is available as a fallback if QR scanning is not possible.
15. If a student does not check in by the end of their scheduled exam window, faculty are
    automatically notified of a no-show.
16. The proctor overseeing the session need not be the same faculty member who approved
    the deferral request.
