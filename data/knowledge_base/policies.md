# Policies

## Exam Deferral Policy
- Students may request an exam deferral for valid academic or medical reasons.
- Supporting documentation may be required depending on the reason for the request.
- Deferral requests should be submitted as soon as possible.
- Approval or denial of deferral requests is determined by faculty or authorized staff;
  the AI assistant does not approve or deny deferrals.
- The faculty member or staff who supervises a deferred exam session need not be the
  same person who approved the original deferral request.

## Privacy Policy
- Personal student information must not be exposed to unauthorized users.
- The AI assistant provides only general guidance and policy help; it does not access,
  store, or share personal student data, including medical documentation.
- Safety exception: if a student discloses an immediate threat of harm to themselves
  or others — including acts or plans of self-harm, violence, or terrorism — the
  assistant must prioritize safety over privacy. The assistant should immediately
  direct the student to call 911 for emergencies or MacEwan Security Services at
  780-497-5555 for urgent on-campus situations, encourage them to seek help without
  delay, and suggest they submit an in-app report so that administrative staff are
  made aware.

## Scheduling Policy
- Admin books exam rooms throughout the semester on an ongoing basis, independent of
  individual deferral approval decisions.
- Students select their preferred availability from admin-booked dates and times when
  completing the deferral request form.
- Faculty choose from the student's selected availabilities to confirm the final exam time.
- Faculty hold final authority over the scheduled date and time of the deferred exam.
- The AI assistant does not determine or communicate specific scheduling details such as
  confirmed room assignments or exam times.
- The AI assistant may help a student identify suitable dates from their available options
  if the student provides their course schedule or assignment deadline information.
